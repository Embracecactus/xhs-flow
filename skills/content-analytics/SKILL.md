# Content analytics

Use for a post, series, weekly, or monthly review when actual platform data, exports, screenshots, or public metrics are available. Do not infer outcomes from a blank template or a local publishing package.

## Validate inputs

Identify platform, content/version, publication date, observation window, field meanings, missing values, and anomalies. Prefer account-owner exports or screenshots, then authorized read-only data. Public metrics can support structural observation but do not reveal private conversion or reach.

Keep exposure, views/reads, engagement, follows, conversion, spend, and production cost distinct. Compare only like platform, content format, and time window; raw cross-platform views are not a ranking.

## Reason from the evidence

Describe the baseline and the strongest observable signal. Test possible contributions from topic/audience, title/cover, first screen or first seconds, structure, timing, tags, and external factors such as promotion or account size.

Correlation is not causation. Mark unsupported causes and small-sample conclusions as pending. Separate commercial from organic content where that affects interpretation.

## Decide and learn

Recommend a bounded action: repeat, repackage, adapt, stop, or collect more data. When the actual evidence supports an experiment, define its hypothesis, one changed variable, success criterion, and observation window. Otherwise mark the review pending and name the missing evidence; do not invent a generic experiment to fill a template. Preserve raw evidence and identify what would falsify the conclusion.

Return data quality, baseline, conclusion confidence, non-conclusions, actions, and the next experiment.
